Every label we re-checked

For Finding 8 we re-checked test labels: the messages where at least 2 of the 4 models agreed on a different category than the Banking77 answer key. Each was judged blind, with the two labels shown in random order. This page lists every verdict, so you can check our calls.

  • Changed (): the alternative is right. Both fit (): either is fair. Original kept () and unclear (): the official label stays.
  • Who decided: Claude drafted every verdict without seeing which label was official or which model said what; the author approved them in bulk, without overrides. The rule was written down before any re-scoring.
  • In the model columns, this style means the model picked the alternative, plain means the original label, and this style means something else.
  • Download: label_changes.csv · re-score yourself with python label_audit.py · results on the overview (Finding 8) and in the technical report.
