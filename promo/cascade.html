Jev vs. the Frontier · The build plan to 99%

82.8% isn't good enough. Here's the build plan to 99%.

Don't chase a better model. Automate only what you can prove is 99% right, then grow that share.

1
Define the target
99% of what the machine decides alone, checked by people.
2
Measure your human ceiling
Two people label the same messages. Disagreement = fuzzy categories.
3
Fix the categories
Merge the ones people confuse. Write tiebreak rules.
4
Run in shadow mode
The AI suggests, people decide. Free labeled data, zero risk.
5
Set a bar per category
Automate a category only where it's 99%+ right. Risky ones stay human.
6
Add checks that don't trust the AI
Keyword rules caught 1 in 7 errors. Check real records too.
7
Turn it on one category at a time
Start with the safest, highest-volume categories.
8
Audit forever, with a brake
Weekly random samples. A slipping category goes back to people.
9
Close the loop
Corrections improve the rules. Re-test before every change.
