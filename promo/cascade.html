Jev vs. the Frontier · The road to 99%

82.8% isn't good enough. Here's the road to 99%.

Automate less, and accuracy climbs. The last step to 99% is system design.

1
Stop automating everything
Pick the accuracy you need. Automate only what clears it.
2
Check the answer key
Jev's 8 top-score "errors" look like labeling mistakes. Fix fuzzy categories.
3
Set the bar per category
A wrong "lost card" answer costs more than a wrong "exchange rate" one.
4
Add cheap rule checks
Plain Python checks already stopped 1 in 7 wrong answers.
5
Learn from people
Every message a person handles teaches the next version.
6
Prove it, then keep checking
Showing 99.9% takes ~3,000 checked decisions with zero mistakes.
